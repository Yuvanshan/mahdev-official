# Backlink strategy

Backlinks cannot be added from code. They are links other sites give us, so this plan lists
where to ask and what to send. The site already exposes the signals Google needs to connect
those links to the brand (Organization and LocalBusiness schema, `sameAs` profiles, matching
name and address). Keep the name, address and phone **identical** everywhere below.

**Name:** MAHDEV Pvt. Ltd. (Mahdev (Pvt) Ltd) · **Site:** the canonical domain in `VITE_SITE_URL`

## Before you start

1. Move to a custom domain first (see `DOMAIN_CONFIGURATION.md`). Links to a `vercel.app` address
   are lost or diluted when the domain changes. Where a redirect is needed, use 301.
2. Link to a specific page, not only the home page: `/sws` for event listings, `/u1` for photography,
   `/it`, `/travels`, `/mart`.

## Phase 1: free profiles (week 1–2)

Each gets the same name, address, phone, short description and a link to the matching division page.

1. Google Business Profile: one for Trincomalee (SWS) and one for Colombo. Add photos and the website link.
2. Facebook page, Instagram, LinkedIn company page, YouTube channel, TikTok. Put the site in each bio.
3. Bing Places and Apple Business Connect.
4. Sri Lankan directories: Yellow Pages Sri Lanka, LankaBusiness-style local listings, chamber of commerce listing.
5. Wedding and event vendor sites that list vendors for free.
6. Photography portfolio sites (for U1 Studio): Behance, 500px, Flickr, each with a link back.

## Phase 2: earned links (month 1–3)

| Target | What to offer | Link to |
|---|---|---|
| Venues and hotels in Trincomalee and Colombo | Credit link on their "preferred vendors" page after an event | `/sws` |
| Couples and clients | Ask for a testimonial post or tagged photo with a link | `/u1`, `/sws` |
| Local wedding and lifestyle bloggers | Free shoot or styled set in exchange for a feature | `/sws`, `/u1` |
| Sri Lankan news and business sites | Press release for a milestone, new division or notable event | `/about` |
| Tourism boards and travel bloggers | Guest guide or partner listing for Mahdev Travels | `/travels` |
| IT and startup communities | Case study or short article from Mahdev IT | `/it` |
| Suppliers and partner brands | Mutual "our partners" links | `/clients` |

## Phase 3: content that earns links (ongoing)

1. One useful guide a month on the Insights page, for example "Wedding decoration costs in Sri Lanka" or
   "How to choose a wedding photographer in Trincomalee".
2. Publish each event or shoot as a project page with real photos and a short story.
3. Share each piece on the social profiles and send it to the venue or client that appears in it.

## Outreach email template

> Subject: Photos from [event] at [venue]
>
> Hello [name], we were at [venue] on [date] for [client]'s [event] and have photos you are welcome to use.
> Our write-up with the full set is here: [project page URL]. If you have a vendor or gallery page,
> we would be glad to be listed. Thank you, [sender], Mahdev

Have the wording reviewed before sending to external contacts.

## What to avoid

- Paid link packages, link farms, comment spam, or hidden footer links. Google can penalise these.
- Different business names or phone numbers across listings.
- Linking with only keyword-heavy text. Mix brand name, page title and plain URLs.

## Tracking

Review monthly in Search Console (Links report) and keep a list.

Aim for 10 quality profile links in month 1, then 2–4 earned links a month.
